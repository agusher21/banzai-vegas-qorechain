HOW IT WORKS
  1. Claim free BNZ from the faucet (1,000 BNZ, once a day, testnet only).
  2. Choose how many tickets (up to 20 per raffle). Each ticket is 5 BNZ. Draws every Sunday, Tuesday and Thursday at 20:00 UTC.
  3. After sales close, anyone can start the draw. Winners are picked from a future blockhash — public and verifiable, no one picks them. 1st gets 30%, 2nd 18%, 3rd 12%, then up to 29 lucky lots of 100 BNZ plus one lot of the remainder.
  4. Won? Claim your prize from "My wins". It never expires.

Testnet only. BNZ has no real value. Contracts: not deployed yet — see README
