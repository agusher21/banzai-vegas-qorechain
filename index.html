HOW IT WORKS
  1. Claim free QOR from the QoreChain faucet (100 QOR per claim, once a day, testnet only).
  2. Choose how many tickets (up to 20 per raffle). Each ticket is 5 QOR. Draws every day at 20:00 UTC, automatic.
  3. After sales close, the draw runs automatically at 20:00 UTC — no one needs to press anything. Winners are picked from a future blockhash — public and verifiable, no one picks them. 1st gets 30%, 2nd 18%, 3rd 12%, then up to 29 lucky lots of 100 QOR plus one lot of the remainder.
  4. Won? Claim your prize from "My wins". It never expires — and it pays out in QOR.

Testnet only. QOR on this testnet has no real value. Contracts: not deployed yet — see README
