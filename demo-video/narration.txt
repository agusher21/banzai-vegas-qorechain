Banzai Vegas is an on-chain raffle on the QoreChain testnet. Buy QOR tickets — five QOR each, up to twenty per round — and win the pot: thirty percent for first place, eighteen for second, twelve for third, plus lucky lots. Draws run every day at twenty hundred UTC, automatic. QOR is free on testnet: the faucet gives you a hundred QOR a day. To play, connect your wallet on the QoreChain testnet and buy your tickets. What makes this post-quantum: the operator runs on an ML-DSA-87 key registered on chain, and every deploy ran inside a hybrid-signed EVM window — secp256k1 plus ML-DSA-87.
